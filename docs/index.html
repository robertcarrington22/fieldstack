Why you can send it

The model never does the math.

Owners and lenders forward this with draw requests. It has to be right, so the parts that can be wrong are computed in code and the model only writes around them.

01 · Computed

Numbers from code, not from a prompt

Aging, dollars, percents, and dates come from deterministic calculations you can audit. The narrative receives them as facts and is told to invent nothing.

02 · Cited

Every claim links to a record

RFI-041, SUB-118.R2, PCO-007. Each citation is a link back to Procore, so an owner's rep can check any sentence in one click.

03 · Approved

A person hits send

Nothing posts back to your systems and nothing goes to the owner on its own. Your PX reads, edits, and sends.
